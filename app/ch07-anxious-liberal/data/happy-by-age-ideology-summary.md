# Happy by Age x Ideology - Summary

Survey-weighted analysis of GSS 1975-2024 (61,949 observations) using svy v0.17.1 (Taylor-series variance, design: stratum=vstrat, psu=vpsu, wgt=wtssps only).

## 1. Youngest-liberal collapse

18-34 Liberals: 27.9% in 1975 -> 18.2% in 2024 (net -9.6 pts).
Long-run pre-2018 mean: 28.0%; 2018-2024 mean: 19.1% (shift: -9.0 pts).
Peak: 38.9% in 1990; trough: 10.5% in 2021 (28.3-point peak-to-trough drop).

## 2. When does the divergence start?

First year 18-34 Liberal falls > 1 SE below the mean of all other cells: **1975** (value=27.9%, others-mean=36.0%, delta=-8.2 pts; |delta| > capped SE (0.90)).

The gap *deepens* notably starting in **2000** -- gap (others-mean - 18-34 Lib) = 11.9 pts vs historical mean+1SD = 11.1 pts (historical mean 6.4, SD 4.7).

## 3. Older conservatives (65+ Conservative)

1990s mean: 40.8%; 2018-2024 mean: 31.4%; shift: -9.4 points.

## 4. Sample-size caveats

Cells (age x ideology) with any year n_actual < 30: 2 groupings.
- 50-64 Liberal: 1 years with n<30 (range 1983-1983)
- 65+ Liberal: 1 years with n<30 (range 1983-1983)

No (year, age, ideology) cells fully missing in the analytic frame.

## 5. Mode-shift check (2018 vs 2022 for 18-34 Liberal)

- 2018 reading: 27.1%
- 2021 reading: 10.5% (web-mode-shift year)
- 2022 reading: 20.4%
- 2024 reading: 18.2%
Reference: pre-2018 mean = 28.0%; 'low' threshold = 23.0% (5 pts below).
2018 is near the long-run mean but 2022 (and 2024) are well below -- the collapse appears post-2021 and is not a one-year artifact.
